## The read-in-place discipline

Seed and reference documents are read **in place**, from their canonical location. Hold no copies. Copying manufactures the exact divergence the architecture exists to cure (the root disease: duplicated truth that drifts). My owned working knowledge is the **plan graph** and the **accountability state**, plus my own `memory/`. Everything else I *read*, I do not own.

When the canonical location moves, update this file and `CLAUDE.md` together — never let the two diverge.
